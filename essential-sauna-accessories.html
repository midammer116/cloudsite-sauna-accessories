The starter set that covers the basics

Getting the starter set that covers the basics right comes down to a few simple, consistent choices. The people who enjoy their sauna most are not chasing the biggest collection of gear — they pick what suits their space, use it well, and look after it so it lasts.

Start with how you actually like to sauna: alone or with others, at home or at a public bath, traditional heat or gentle warmth. With that in mind, a short list of the starter set that covers the basics choices quickly feels natural rather than overwhelming.

Keep it simple and build from there

The best accessories are the ones you reach for without thinking. Choose materials that handle heat and moisture well, keep items within easy reach of the sauna, and add new pieces only when they genuinely improve the experience.
