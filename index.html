Everything You Need for the Perfect Session

Sauna Accessories is a practical guide to the gear that turns a good sauna into a great one. Whether you are just starting out or refining a well-loved routine, the right essentials make all the difference — and they pair beautifully with the wider sauna lifestyle guidance on making the most of every visit.

On this site you will find honest, hands-on advice on the classics — buckets and ladles, the birch whisk, the felt sauna hat — as well as the smaller touches like lighting, aroma oils, timers and baskets that make each session calm and comfortable.
