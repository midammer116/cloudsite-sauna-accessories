The right towel for the job

Getting the right towel for the job right comes down to a few simple, consistent choices. The people who enjoy their sauna most are not chasing the biggest collection of gear — they pick what suits their space, use it well, and look after it so it lasts.

Start with how you actually like to sauna: alone or with others, at home or at a public bath, traditional heat or gentle warmth. With that in mind, a short list of the right towel for the job choices quickly feels natural rather than overwhelming.
