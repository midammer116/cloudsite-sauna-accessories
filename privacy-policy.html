Overview

This privacy policy explains how Sauna Accessories handles information when you visit this informational website. We keep things simple: we do not require an account, and we do not sell personal data.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or provide personal details to browse the site. Like most websites, our hosting provider may record standard, technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, for security and analytics purposes.

How we use information

Any technical information collected is used to keep the site secure, understand how it is used, and improve the content. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Cookies and third parties

Some pages embed media — such as videos and social posts — from third-party services like YouTube, X and Pinterest. These services may set their own cookies and process data under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the policies of those services directly.

Your choices

You can browse without providing personal information, and you can clear cookies through your browser settings at any time.

If you have any questions about this policy, please reach out via the contact details shown on the host of this site.
